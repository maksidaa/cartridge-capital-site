Swoll Bro

Privacy policy and support. Effective October 26, 2026.

The short version

Swoll Bro does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no chat. The developer never receives any data about you, your child or how you play. The game works offline; when you're online it checks this website for game updates.

Your saved game

Your stars, sizes and Swole Bucks, and each player's name and icon, are saved on your device. If iCloud is turned on for your Apple Account, a copy is also kept in your own iCloud storage so you can continue on another device. That copy is stored by Apple and the developer cannot see it. Deleting the app removes the save from your device. You can remove the iCloud copy in your device's iCloud settings.

Game updates

Now and then the game checks this website for an update to the game and downloads it. That request contains no personal information and no identifiers. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, can see the IP address a request comes from; we don't receive or store it.

Purchases

The optional "Full Game" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Before the App Store's purchase sheet appears, the game asks a grown-ups-only question so a child can't start a purchase by accident. The developer does not receive your name, email, payment details or any other personal information.

Children

Swoll Bro is made for kids and families. It collects no information from anyone, including children, and it has no ads, no links out of the app and no way to talk to other players.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email adgoodwin82@gmail.com. Please include your device model and what happened.

Lost the full game after reinstalling? Open the road map, tap The Gym, then tap "Restore purchase." (Or tap "Full game" on the title screen.) Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.
