Privacy Policy

Cartridge Capital for iPhone · Effective October 10, 2026

HomeSupport

The short version

Cartridge Capital does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game works offline.

What happens to your information

Your saved game

Your company is saved on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on for your Apple ID, Apple also keeps a copy in your iCloud account so you can continue on another device. We cannot see or access this data.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your net worth and legacy score to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, shown under your Game Center name. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy. You can turn Game Center off in Settings.

Purchases

The full-game purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple only tells the game whether the full game is unlocked.

No third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code, and it does not connect to any server of ours.

Children

Because the game collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children under 13.

Deleting your data

Tap Start over in Company › Overview › Game to erase your company, or delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage on your iPhone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions? Use the contact link on our Support page.
