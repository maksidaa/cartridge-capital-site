Common questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap the gear next to the speed buttons at the top of the screen to open Settings, then tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Can my family play the full game?

Yes. The full game supports Family Sharing. Anyone in your Apple family group gets it without buying it again. They may need to tap Restore purchase once.

Does my company save across devices?

Your game saves every few seconds on your iPhone. If iCloud is on for your Apple ID, a copy is kept in iCloud so you can continue on another device signed in to the same account.

How do I start over?

Tap the gear to open Settings and tap Start over. Your old company is replaced with a new one, on this device and in iCloud. Your purchase stays.

What does the free version include?

The garage and the loft, with some limits on stores, buildings, media, executives and startups. The full game opens the Studio floor and everything after it: bigger games, consoles, Wall Street, the Frontier and your family dynasty.
